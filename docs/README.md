# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A central entry point to OctoAcme's project management guidance.

## Project management processes at a glance

OctoAcme uses an iterative lifecycle that begins with **initiation** to validate the business need, align stakeholders, define measurable success criteria, and decide whether to proceed. **Planning** turns approved work into prioritized, estimated backlog items with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, dependencies, and risks.

During **execution and tracking**, delivery teams work in small increments through a project board and pull-request workflow. Regular standups, delivery syncs, demos, and status updates keep work visible; risks and blockers are tracked and escalated from the team to the PM, Product Lead, or sponsor as needed. The PM coordinates delivery, schedules, and communications, while the Product Manager owns outcomes and prioritization. Developers deliver and test the solution, QA validates quality and acceptance, and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Quality is built into delivery. Teams use unit and integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, manual QA when needed, and CI-based test, lint, and security checks. **Release and deployment** requires completed acceptance criteria, passing checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback or incident procedures. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, **retrospectives and continuous improvement** capture learnings and track owners, due dates, and success criteria for prioritized follow-up actions.
